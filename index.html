Let’s Go Dive Compass

A magnetic compass for Apple Watch Ultra.

Support

For questions, feedback, or technical support, email gmiscow@gmail.com.

Please include your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Review diagnostic records before sharing them; do not send passwords or other secrets.

Using the compass

Use the Action button to mark a bearing. Once a bearing is marked, rotate the Digital Crown to choose a reciprocal bearing, a 90° turn left or right, or clear the bearing. Confirm using the Action button. The selection closes after five seconds without confirmation.

The arrow and text show the correction to the marked bearing. A checkmark indicates alignment within 5°. Colors provide additional cues.

Press and hold the compass screen for options, local history, and help. With VoiceOver, use the “Options and history” action.

The heading is magnetic. Nearby magnets and metal can affect readings. This app does not measure depth or calculate decompression.

Diagnostic history

History is stored locally on the watch and is not sent automatically to the developer. The local history keeps up to 100,000 events and discards events older than seven days as new events are recorded. You may voluntarily share diagnostics when requesting support.

If requested for troubleshooting, a Mac with Xcode or compatible Apple device-management tools may be used to download a copy of the app’s data container. Do not uninstall the app before preserving records you want to keep.

Suporte em português

Para dúvidas e suporte, escreva para gmiscow@gmail.com. Informe o modelo do relógio, as versões do watchOS e do app e uma descrição do problema.

Marque um rumo pelo botão de Ação. Gire a Digital Crown para escolher uma ação e confirme pelo botão de Ação. A seta, o texto e a marca de confirmação permitem interpretar a orientação sem depender apenas das cores.

A direção é magnética. O app não mede profundidade nem calcula descompressão. Os diagnósticos ficam no relógio e não são enviados automaticamente ao desenvolvedor.
