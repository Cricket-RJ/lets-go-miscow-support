Privacy Policy

Let’s Go Dive Compass · Effective October 18, 2026

Information handled by the app

The app uses magnetic heading and sensor-quality readings, app/session state, and Water Lock state to provide compass functions and local diagnostics. It does not request or record geographic coordinates. It uses watchOS underwater-session capabilities to support use in water.

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-operated server. It does not automatically transmit app readings or diagnostic history to the developer.

On-device diagnostics and retention

Local diagnostic history includes timestamps, magnetic headings, sensor uncertainty, watchOS/app versions, session events, and screen-state changes. It keeps up to 100,000 events and discards events older than seven days as new events are recorded. This is not a guarantee that a complete seven-day history is available.

The app also writes diagnostic events to Apple’s system logging facilities; watchOS controls their storage and retention separately. Apple’s device, backup, and diagnostic services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local data from the watch; this does not remove separately saved copies, backups, emails, or system records managed by Apple. There is no developer-hosted copy of your app history unless you choose to share one.

When you contact support

If you choose to email support or share a diagnostic file, the developer receives your email address, message, and any files you send. These are used to respond to and investigate your request, not for advertising or tracking. To request deletion of support correspondence or attachments held by the developer, email gmiscow@gmail.com.

Política de privacidade em português

O app usa direção magnética, estimativa de qualidade do sensor, estado da interface e da sessão e estado do Water Lock. Não solicita nem registra coordenadas geográficas. Não tem contas, anúncios, rastreamento, análise de terceiros ou servidor do desenvolvedor e não transmite automaticamente leituras ou histórico ao desenvolvedor.

O histórico local contém horários, direções, incerteza do sensor, versões do app/watchOS e eventos de sessão e tela. Mantém até 100 mil eventos e descarta eventos com mais de sete dias conforme novos eventos são registrados. O watchOS administra separadamente os registros do sistema. Serviços de backup e diagnóstico da Apple seguem suas configurações e as políticas da Apple.

Desinstalar o app remove seus dados locais do relógio, mas não cópias exportadas, backups, e-mails ou registros administrados pela Apple. Se você enviar uma solicitação de suporte, o desenvolvedor receberá seu e-mail, mensagem e anexos para responder e investigar o problema. Para solicitar a exclusão desse material em poder do desenvolvedor, entre em contato por gmiscow@gmail.com.
